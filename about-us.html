About EduPulse AI

A place to practise.
A reason to keep learning.

Our purpose is simple: make active revision easier to fit into your study routine.

Learning through practice

EduPulse AI brings short practice sessions and timed mock papers into one browser-based workspace. It is designed for learners who want to attempt questions, compare explanations and identify topics that need another look. Teachers and parents can also use its practice prompts to support revision.

What the tools actually do

Practice uses a small built-in topic library and general templates to assemble questions. Its feedback checks key terms rather than understanding every explanation. Paper Cracker organises practice questions into exam-style sections with a timer and model solutions. No live generative AI model is connected in this version.

Honest preparation, realistic expectations

We encourage independent attempts, thoughtful review and checking facts against reliable textbooks. EduPulse AI does not provide leaked papers, official marking or guaranteed grades. The available class and subject options are not a claim of complete curriculum coverage. Use the tools alongside your schoolwork and the latest official exam guidance.

Study guide

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers about using EduPulse AI for revision and exam preparation.

What is EduPulse AI?

EduPulse AI is a free browser-based study tool for topic practice and mock exam preparation. It offers questions, hints, model answers, keyword-based feedback and timed practice papers.

How do I practise questions on EduPulse AI?

Open Practice, enter a topic, select a study level and choose the number of questions. Try answering before checking the feedback, then compare your work with the model answer and revise any missing ideas.

Which subjects and topics can I study?

The built-in library covers Photosynthesis, Quadratic Equations, Newton’s Laws of Motion, the French Revolution, Verbs & Tenses, and Fractions. The interface also offers broader subject and class selections, but these do not mean every syllabus is covered. Topics outside the library use general templates.

Can I create timed mock exam papers?

Yes. Open Paper Cracker, choose your class or exam, subject and chapter, then generate a practice paper. Standard mode offers 50 marks in 90 minutes; the more challenging mode offers 100 marks in three hours. Papers include MCQs, short answers and higher-order thinking questions.

Does EduPulse AI use a live AI model to mark answers?

No. This version compares key terms in your answer with a built-in model answer. It can miss valid explanations or award credit for keywords without full understanding. Use it as a revision aid and ask a teacher when an answer is unclear.

Are the mock papers official or guaranteed to match my exam?

No. EduPulse AI is an independent practice resource, not an exam board or an official source of exam questions. Class, JEE and NEET selections help organise practice; they do not certify syllabus coverage, predict exam questions or guarantee results.

Is EduPulse AI free, and do I need an account?

The current tools are free to use and do not require an account or payment. You can start practising directly in your browser.

Are my answers and scores saved?

The current app keeps answers and scores only in the active browser session and has no account-based progress history. Refreshing, leaving the page or switching tools can clear your work. The app does not send your typed answers to an AI service.

Can I print a mock paper or save it as a PDF?

Yes. Generate a paper in Paper Cracker and select Print / Save as PDF. Your browser opens its print dialog, where you can choose a printer or a PDF destination if supported. Show or hide solutions before printing to suit your revision.

How can I use practice questions effectively for exam preparation?

Start with a topic from your current syllabus, answer without looking at the solution, and review the ideas you missed. Revisit your textbook, try again and use timed papers to practise pacing. Combine the tool with teacher guidance and official syllabus materials.
