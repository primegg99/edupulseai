Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy describes the current EduPulse AI website and its browser-based study tools.

Practice answers and session data

The current study app processes topic entries, written answers, selections and scores in your browser. It does not include an account system, a submission endpoint or a database for saving your practice history, and does not send your answers to a live AI provider. This information is held in the page’s temporary memory and may be lost when you refresh, leave the page or switch tools. Avoid entering names, contact details or other sensitive information into study fields.

Hosting and technical requests

The site is hosted on Netlify. Loading the website sends technical request information, such as your IP address, browser information and the requested URL, to the hosting service. Hosting providers may process request logs for delivery, security and service operation. Their retention practices are governed by their own policies; we do not promise that all technical information disappears when you close a tab.

Third-party resources

The practice page loads fonts from Google Fonts and software resources from jsDelivr and the Tailwind CSS CDN. Your browser connects to those services, which receive technical request data such as your IP address and browser details. Their processing is subject to their own privacy policies. See Netlify’s privacy policy, Google’s privacy policy, jsDelivr’s privacy policy and Tailwind’s privacy policy.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The current application code does not set cookies, use local storage for study history, or include analytics or advertising scripts. Third-party resource requests remain subject to the providers’ practices. If analytics, advertising or accounts are introduced, this policy should be updated and any legally required notice or consent provided before those features operate.

Printing and your choices

You can clear the current study session by closing or refreshing the page. Printed papers and PDFs are handled by your browser, device and selected print destination; you control any copies you save. Browser privacy settings can restrict third-party resources, although doing so may prevent the practice app from loading.

Younger learners

These educational tools may be used by school-age learners. The current app does not ask for personal profiles or knowingly collect personal details through its study fields. Younger learners should use the site with appropriate parent, guardian or teacher guidance and should not enter personal information.

Privacy rights and policy changes

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data, or raise a concern with your local data protection authority. The current site has no account records or server-stored answer history to retrieve or delete. For technical data processed by service providers, consult their linked privacy policies for rights and contact information. Changes to this policy are shown on this page with an updated date.
